Lindos

Welcome to Lindos

A familiar desktop for people coming from Windows: a Start menu, a taskbar, dark by default, and the keyboard shortcuts you already know.

  • Lindos is Linux. It is not Windows and never pretends to be.
  • Set-up happens once, when your PC restarts – you choose how it should work.
  • Everything on these slides works without an internet connection.

Five Modes, one choice

Choose in Setup. Change any time in Lindos Settings.

  • EverydayBalanced: browsing, media and daily tasks
  • GamingMaximum performance, game launchers pinned
  • WorkOffice and mail at hand, calm power profile
  • CreatorCreative apps and Windows-app recipes
  • LiteFor older PCs and 4 GB of RAM

Bring your Windows programs

Double-click an .exe or .msi installer and Lindos opens it with Wine or Proton, then adds the program to your Start menu.

  • Wine and Proton translate Windows programs – there is no Windows inside, and no virtual machine.
  • Most everyday programs and many games work. Software that installs Windows drivers does not.
  • When a file cannot run, Lindos says so plainly and suggests what to use instead.

Made for gaming

Steam with Proton and Lutris are set up for you. Heroic, Prism Launcher (Minecraft) and Sober (Roblox) install in one click. GameMode and MangoHud are included.

Honest note: some online games use kernel-level anti-cheat that no Linux supports. Valorant and Fortnite do not run on any Linux, Lindos included. Before you buy a multiplayer game, check protondb.com and areweanticheatyet.com.

Private by design

No telemetry, no advertising, no crash uploads, and no account needed to use your own computer.

  • Firefox is included. Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge are downloaded from their publishers when you choose them – they are never bundled.
  • Moving from Windows? Transfer from Windows can bring over your files, browser bookmarks and a list of your programs. It only ever reads your Windows partition.

What happens next

When the installation finishes, remove the installation medium and restart. Lindos Setup then guides you through:

  1. Mode
  2. Browser
  3. Look and feel
  4. Apps
  5. Privacy

Setup also works offline. You can skip anything and change it later in Lindos Settings.

Installing Lindos. This can take a few minutes – please keep your PC plugged in.

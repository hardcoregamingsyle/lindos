Lindos - legal and open-source notices
======================================

This text is shown by Settings > About > "Legal and open-source notices".  It is information, not legal advice,
and it is not exhaustive: the licence of every installed package is in /usr/share/doc/<package>/copyright.

Lindos itself
-------------
Lindos's own code, packaging, artwork (wallpapers, logo, icons) and documentation are free software, licensed
GPL-3.0-or-later.  They come with no warranty, to the extent the law permits.  Source:
https://github.com/hardcoregamingsyle/lindos

What Lindos is built on
-----------------------
Lindos is an independent project.  Its base system is made of packages from Ubuntu 24.04 LTS ("noble"), from Debian,
and from the Linux Mint 22.x repositories and installer tooling: the Linux kernel, systemd, apt and dpkg, the GNU
tools, X.Org, the Xfce desktop, LightDM and thousands of other programs.  Each keeps its own licence (mostly GPL,
LGPL, MIT, BSD, MPL and Apache).  The source code of every one of them is available from the archive it came from
(apt source <package>).

Names and trademarks
--------------------
Linux Mint, Ubuntu, Debian, Firefox, Windows, Steam, Xfce and the other product names in this text belong to their
owners.  Lindos is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of them.  Lindos is not Windows and does not
contain Windows: Windows programs run through Wine and Proton, a compatibility layer, without a virtual machine.
Some programs (in particular games with kernel-level anti-cheat) do not run.
